# Collections & Recoveries AI Review Assurance Toolkit

A fictional demonstration of how AI can support quality assurance for UK collections and recoveries case reviews.

## What this project demonstrates

This toolkit shows how an AI-assisted assurance layer could review an agent's completed rationale against the evidence recorded in a fictional customer case and highlight points that may need further investigation.

It is designed to demonstrate:

- Collections & recoveries case-review assurance
- Consumer Duty outcome testing
- Vulnerability and support-needs identification
- Evidence-versus-rationale checks
- CONC / FCA guidance cross-referencing
- Human-in-the-loop challenge and override
- Management information and thematic trend reporting

## Example workflow

1. An agent reviews a fictional customer interaction and records their rationale.
2. The toolkit compares the rationale with case evidence and customer circumstances.
3. Potential gaps or inconsistencies are highlighted.
4. Relevant FCA / Consumer Duty considerations are surfaced.
5. A human reviewer decides whether the case can be passed, needs further work, or should be escalated.
6. Dashboard MI shows recurring themes across the sample population.

## Important

This is a **personal portfolio project built with AI assistance using fictional cases only**.

It does not contain real customer data, confidential information, proprietary firm methodology, or production AI integrations. The project is intended to demonstrate remediation, outcomes-testing and AI assurance concepts for recruiters and financial-services professionals.

## Regulatory references

The demo is designed around public FCA material, including:

- FCA Consumer Duty
- PRIN 2A
- CONC 7: Arrears, default and recovery
- FCA vulnerability guidance

The interface uses simplified summaries for demonstration purposes and is not legal or compliance advice.
